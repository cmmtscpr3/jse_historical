First-Principles Engine — 2018 → 2022 Backtest

Out-of-sample evaluation of first_principles_prediction_engine.py on the 2022 Johor DUN election (3-way BN vs PH vs PN), assuming perfect knowledge of the 2022 within-group BN support coefficients (BM, BC), Malay/Chinese turnout rates, and 2022 racial composition — with the 2018 seat residuals carried forward as the only out-of-sample ingredient. Predictions are produced by the engine's own predict() code path, identical to the dashboard's JS engine.
